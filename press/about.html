About Double Take Press

Double Take Press is an independent literary and artistic initiative within Double Take World, drawn to the absurd, the conceptual, the irreverent, and the unexpected. We welcome works that challenge conventions, expose contradictions, and invite a second look.

Our interests span poetry, experimental prose, translation, visual art, and interdisciplinary work. We currently focus on English and Chinese, but welcome other languages, too.

關於我們

且慢文字社是且慢旗下的獨立文學與藝術出版計畫。 我們鍾情於荒誕、觀念、反叛與出人意料的創作， 歡迎那些挑戰慣例、揭示矛盾，並引人重新審視的作品。

我們關注詩歌、實驗性散文、翻譯、視覺藝術及跨領域創作。 目前以中文與英文為主，也歡迎其他語言的作品。
