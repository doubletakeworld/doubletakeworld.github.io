About Double Take Press

Nonsense, justified.

Double Take Press is an independent literary and artistic initiative within Double Take World. We are drawn to the absurd, the conceptual, the irreverent, and the difficult-to-categorize.

We welcome writing and art that invite a second look: works that challenge familiar forms, expose contradictions, or turn a small idea into an unexpected question.

Our editorial direction includes poetry, experimental prose, translation, visual art, and interdisciplinary work. We aim to make space for voices working across languages and conventions.

關於我們

Double Take Press 是 Double Take World 旗下的獨立文學與藝術出版計畫。我們關注荒誕、觀念寫作、實驗詩歌、翻譯與跨媒介創作，歡迎讓讀者重新觀看日常與語言的作品。
